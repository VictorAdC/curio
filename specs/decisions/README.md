# Decision Records

This folder stores concise architectural or product decisions that may need historical context.

Use it for decisions such as:

- why local-first persistence was chosen;
- why Zustand was selected;
- why the app starts without a backend;
- when IndexedDB should be introduced.

## Suggested Convention

- One file per decision.
- Prefix with a sequence number, for example `001-local-first.md`.
- Keep each record short and focused on context, decision, and consequences.
